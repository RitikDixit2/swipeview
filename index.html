    Questions

    Is Swipe View free?

    Yes. It's free to download from Google Play.

    Does it need the internet?

    No. Your videos, contacts, apps, sums and alarms all work offline. Web searches open in your browser or the app you pick.

    Why would it ask for my contacts?

    Only if you turn on contact search. Your contacts are searched on your phone and never uploaded.

    Can I move my data to a new phone?

    Yes. Back up your favourites, saved links and settings to a folder you choose, then restore them on the new phone.

    Does it work on iPhone?

    Not yet. Swipe View is for Android.
